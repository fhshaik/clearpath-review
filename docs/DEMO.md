# Five-minute presentation

## 1. Start with the bottleneck (30 seconds)

"ClearPath's reviewers are coordinating marketing approvals in Excel and email.
My hypothesis is that throughput is limited by gathering context, finding issues,
explaining corrections, and tracking the latest version. This workspace keeps
that context with each campaign. AI drafts the review; the person makes the decision."

## 2. Review a submission (90 seconds)

Open **A little breathing room / BrightMoney**. Identify the absolute approval
claim, rate-disclosure concern, and missing partner disclosure. Click a highlight
to connect evidence with its finding and reference.

State clearly: "The initial workspace contains curated sample analyses. Live AI
is available when a visitor connects their own OpenRouter account."

If demonstrating live AI, connect your account through **Connect AI → Continue with OpenRouter**,
authorize the app, and click **Run live AI review** before recording finding dispositions.
OpenRouter credits and key allowances apply. Do not claim the live integration
has been verified unless this step succeeds with a real visitor connection.

## 3. Close the review loop (90 seconds)

Click **Request changes**. Show the editable feedback, which is recorded in the
workspace rather than sent by email. Open the suggested revision as a Marketing
draft, edit if needed, and submit version 2. Compare the two complete texts and
show that version 1 still retains its change request.

Switch the demo role to Compliance. Review the corrected version, click
**Approve version 2**, and confirm the human review attestation.

## 4. Show durable evidence (30 seconds)

Open Activity. Show who did what and which version it concerned. Export the review
record. Reload to show that records are saved to the server, not only page state.

## 5. Explain scope and tradeoffs (60 seconds)

- The checklist is illustrative, not comprehensive regulatory certification.
- AI cannot approve, publish, change copy automatically, or execute tools.
- AI credentials are encrypted server-side and expire after 24 hours. Copy goes
  to OpenRouter and its model provider only when a review is requested.
- No-login roles are a demo convenience; production needs real identity and RBAC.
- Anonymous sessions have separate workspaces. Sharing the URL does not share data.
- Text review cannot assess disclosure placement in a real image or webpage.

Proposed measurement: instrument time to first review, reviewer handling time,
revision count, and submission-to-decision time by product and channel. Do not
claim a measured speed improvement from a seeded prototype.
