# Five-minute demo and self-serve path

Live app: https://clearpath-review.vercel.app

## Prepare

Open the app on a desktop. If your own workspace contains prior experiments, use
Reset demo to restore the fictional campaigns. Reset does not replenish included
AI allowance. Every browser gets a separate saved workspace; the visitor
will not see your edits merely by opening your URL.

The opening examples are labeled SAMPLE. Live results vary, so demonstrate the
reasoning and workflow rather than memorizing a fixed count of findings.

## 1. Frame the bottleneck and open a review — 45 seconds

Say: “ClearPath coordinates reviews in Excel and email. My hypothesis is that
reviewers lose time gathering context, explaining corrections, and tracking the
latest version. This workspace keeps that work with the campaign.”

Show queue status, priority, product, affiliate, and launch target. Click **Open
sample review** to open **A little breathing room / BrightMoney**.

## 2. Run real AI and inspect evidence — 75 seconds

Click **Run live AI review** before confirming or dismissing any findings. While
waiting, explain that fictional creative is sent to Anthropic, not automatically
published. Sponsored reviews require no provider signup while allowance remains.

After completion, point out **LIVE AI**, the model and timestamp. Inspect the
approval promise, rate context, and affiliate disclosure concern. Click a
highlight to connect the exact source language to its finding and policy
reference. The model may identify an additional funding-timing concern.

Say: “These are potential concerns, not a legal certification. Evidence has to
match the source text. AI can propose a correction, but it cannot approve.”

Show that **Approve version 1** is disabled while findings remain unresolved.
If useful, click **Confirm issue** once to show reviewer judgment.

## 3. Request changes and submit a Marketing revision — 75 seconds

Click **Request changes**. Read or edit **Feedback for Marketing**, then click
**Request changes** in the dialog. Feedback is recorded here; no email is sent.

Expand **Review proposed changes** under Suggested revision. Click **Open as
Marketing draft**. That switches the demo role to Marketing and opens editable
proposed copy. Check that the guarantee is removed, rate language is supported or
removed, and relationship/eligibility language is appropriate. Review the model's
wording rather than accepting it solely because it was generated.

Enter **What changed?**, then click **Submit new version**. Submitting creates a
new review. It does not publish to a marketing channel.

## 4. Compare and record a human decision — 75 seconds

The app opens **Compare versions**. Show the exact old and new text, additions and
removals, and version 1's original change request in **Version history**.

Switch **Demo role → Compliance**. If a full minute has elapsed and allowance
remains, an optional second live review can inspect the corrected version. Basic
checks also run on every new version. Resolve any remaining concern through a
correction or a dismissal supported by a reason; do not dismiss a finding merely
to force the demo to finish.

Click **Approve version 2**, explain the human attestation, and click **Record
approval** as part of this fictional demonstration. With real creative, the
reviewer would need the actual placement and offer evidence before attesting.

## 5. Show saved evidence and explain scope — 30 seconds

Open **Activity**, show the request, revision and approval, and click **Export
record**. Reload to show that saved records survive page state.

Say: “I would measure reviewer handling time, revisions per campaign, and time to
decision by product and channel. This prototype demonstrates the mechanism; I
haven't measured a throughput improvement.”

## What a self-serve visitor should do

1. Open the URL; no login or key is needed for the sample workflow.
2. Click Open sample review and, when available, Run live AI review.
3. Inspect evidence and request changes.
4. Open the proposed Marketing draft, submit it, and compare versions.
5. Switch to Compliance, record a supported decision, and inspect/export activity.

## Recovery paths

| Situation | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Included review just ran | Wait one minute; inspect evidence or versions in the meantime. |
| Included allowance exhausted | Continue the curated sample workflow or connect your own OpenRouter account. |
| Provider fails | Saved work stays unchanged. The failed attempt still consumes included allowance. |
| AI draft introduces new numbers | The draft is withheld; write a revision from verified source terms. |
| Another tab changed the workspace | The app refreshes current state; review it before trying again. |
| Approval is disabled | An issue remains open/confirmed. Correct it or record a reasoned dismissal. |
| No proposed revision | Draft as Marketing using the findings; proposed copy is optional. |

## What to say plainly

Role switching simulates colleagues; it is not production authentication.
The checklist is illustrative, text review cannot assess visual placement, and
activity is preserved by the API rather than an independently immutable ledger.
The optional OpenRouter grant remains separately unverified unless a real
visitor completes it. The sponsored Anthropic path has its own live evidence.
